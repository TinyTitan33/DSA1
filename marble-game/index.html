The invariant

Instead of tracking everything, watch just the white marbles. Here is how each kind of pick changes the white count:

two white: white count drops by 2
two black: white count drops by 0
one of each: white count drops by 0
Every move changes the white count by 0 or −2. Subtracting 2 never turns odd into even, so the parity (odd or even) of the white count never changes. That is an invariant: something that stays true after every step.
